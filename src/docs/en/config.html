About Character Setup

Customize your AI companion through "Character Setup" and make them truly unique.

Setup options

  • Name: Give them a name, which the AI will adopt as its identity.
  • Gender: Choose "Boy" or "Girl".
  • Voice: Pick the voice (EdgeTTS) they speak with; see the list below.
  • Description: Describe their personality, role, hobbies, etc., e.g. "gentle, caring, loves reading".

Saving and applying

Click "Back" when finished and confirm to save. The new settings take effect immediately for subsequent conversations.

Tip: If you see "some fields are empty", check for missing required fields; if you see "the selected voice is unavailable", pick a valid voice from the list.
